Privacy policy

This document describes exactly what this site does with your data. It is short because the site collects little.

Last updated : 2026-09-15

Data controller

TLEMCENYA, National road N7, Tlemcen 13000, Algeria. For any question about your data: export@tlemcenya.dz.

Processing is governed by Algerian law no. 18-07 of 10 June 2018 on the protection of natural persons in the processing of personal data. If you write to us from the European Union, the GDPR applies to this processing as well.

What we collect, and nothing else

If you place an order (shop page): full name, phone, wilaya, commune, delivery address, and the optional note you add. Plus the contents of your order.

If you request a quotation (export page): company, contact name, email, phone, country, format, volume, Incoterm and your message.

That is all. The site asks for no date of birth, no identity document and no bank details — payment is in cash to the courier.

Why

  • To call you back and confirm the order, the lead time and the delivery charge.
  • To prepare and deliver the order.
  • To answer a quotation request and draw up an offer.
  • To keep a record of a transaction, as commercial law requires.

Your data is never sold, rented or used for advertising. We will not send you a commercial message without your explicit agreement.

How long we keep it

Unconfirmed order
3 months, then deleted
Delivered order
10 years — the statutory retention period for commercial records
Quotation request with no follow-up
2 years, then deleted
Basket in your browser
30 days, then cleared automatically

The basket is not kept by us: it stays in your own browser and you can clear it at any time by clearing the site's data.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. None for analytics, none for advertising, none for social networks.

It uses a single local-storage entry (tly.cart.v1) to remember your basket between pages. That entry never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and cannot identify you: it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for.

If an analytics tool is ever added, this page will be updated and a consent banner will appear before anything is stored.

Who else has access

No data is sent to a third party to display a page: the typefaces are hosted on our own server, and there is no embedded map, no external video and no social network button.

  • The courier, who receives only the name, phone and address needed for the delivery.
  • The site host, which keeps technical connection logs.
  • The form delivery service, where applicable — @to be named once the provider is chosen.

Transfers outside Algeria

If the form is carried by a provider established abroad, your data passes through its servers. The chosen provider and its country of establishment will be named here. While no provider is configured, requests leave through your own mail software and pass through no intermediary.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts everything you type between your device and the server. There is no public database: the site is made of static files, with no administration area and no user account to compromise.

Orders received are kept in an email account protected by a password and two-factor authentication.

Your rights

You may ask to access your data, have it corrected, have it erased, or object to its processing. Write to export@tlemcenya.dz: we answer within thirty days.

In Algeria you may refer the matter to the national personal data protection authority. If you live in the European Union, you may refer it to your country's supervisory authority.

This document was drafted from how the site actually works. It does not replace review by a lawyer, who alone can confirm that the obligations of law 18-05 on electronic commerce are covered.
