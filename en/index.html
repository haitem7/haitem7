The mill

We show the room as it is, unstaged. A buyer committing to a container has a right to see where the oil is made, and a well-kept room reads more convincingly in an ordinary photograph than in a studio shot.

Oil at the decanter outlet, before filtering: that dense, cloudy green is what an olive pressed on the day it was picked gives.
