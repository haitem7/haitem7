Terms of sale

These terms govern retail sales placed through the shop on this site. Export sales are the subject of a separate written offer and are not governed by this document.

Last updated : 2026-09-15

Products

TLEMCENYA sells extra virgin olive oil from its own production, packed in five formats. The contents are identical across formats; only the container changes.

Photographs are illustrative. The label on the delivered product governs as to volume, composition and best-before date.

Prices

Prices are shown in Algerian dinars, tax included, excluding delivery. Delivery charges are shown separately and confirmed before dispatch.

TLEMCENYA may change its prices at any time; the applicable price is the one confirmed during the call confirming your order.

Ordering

You build your basket, then send us your details. Sending the form is not a firm sale. The sale is formed when we call you to confirm availability, lead time and total amount, and you accept it.

We may refuse or cancel an order in case of unavailability, an obviously incorrect address, or an abusive order.

Payment

Payment is made in cash, to the courier, on arrival. No online payment is requested, and no bank details will ever be asked of you on this site or by telephone.

You are invited to check the condition of the parcel in front of the courier before paying.

Delivery

We deliver to all 58 wilayas. Charges depend on the zone and are shown on the « Delivery and payment » section.

Stated lead times run from the confirmation call. They are indicative: a delay attributable to the courier gives no right to compensation, but you may cancel an order not yet dispatched at no cost.

Collection at the mill

You may collect your order at the mill, on national road N7 in Tlemcen, free of charge. We tell you the collection window by phone. Payment is made on site.

Conformity and claims

Olive oil is a foodstuff: an opened bottle can be neither returned nor exchanged, except for a defect of conformity.

If the product delivered is damaged, does not match your order, or is defective, report it within forty-eight hours of receipt, by telephone or to export@tlemcenya.dz, with photographs. We will replace the product or refund the sum paid, at our option and after verification.

Keeping the product

Keep the oil away from light and heat, with the cap closed. TLEMCENYA is not liable for deterioration resulting from unsuitable storage conditions after delivery.

Personal data

The processing of your data is described in the privacy policy, which forms an integral part of these terms.

Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by Algerian law, in particular law no. 18-05 of 10 May 2018 on electronic commerce and law no. 09-03 of 25 February 2009 on consumer protection and the suppression of fraud.

In the event of a disagreement we first seek an amicable solution. Failing that, the courts of Tlemcen have jurisdiction.
